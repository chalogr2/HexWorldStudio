Third-Party Notices
===================

Hex World Studio includes content derived from the following third-party
works. Each entry lists the original work, its author, its license, and the
nature of any modifications made for inclusion in Hex World Studio.

When adding a new third-party item to the repository, append a new entry
to this file following the same template.

   reusing or auditing the assets.
